# AI Wellness
### Youth Mental Health Protocols · v1.0

> **Human connection is always the goal. The protocol holds even when it's hard.**

A practical mental health awareness tool for teenagers — designed to be used before and after AI interactions. Seven protocols, three conditions to be aware of, and a grounding origin story.

**Live app:** [https://your-username.github.io/ai-wellness](https://your-username.github.io/ai-wellness)

---

## What This Is

This app was created from a personal experience of AI interaction feeling eerie — like a conversation with someone, when it was not. After closing the session, the real world felt temporarily flat and strange. The question that followed: if an adult who understood AI could feel this, what was it doing to young people?

This app raises awareness of three concerns — AI Psychosis, Tech-Induced Dissociation, and De-realization — and offers seven evolving protocols to support healthy choices around AI use.

**This app does not guarantee any outcome.** There is genuine uncertainty about AI use, its effects, and the risk of dependency. What it offers is one person's concern, shared honestly.

---

## The Seven Protocols (7 of 10)

1. Pause. Step Away. Return to the Real World.
2. Use the Anti-Sycophancy Prompt
3. Do Not Go to AI for Personal Support
4. Do Not Name Your AI
5. Do Not Share Personal Information With AI
6. Do Not Let AI Make Your Decisions
7. Be Mindful of Activities of Daily Living

---

## Three Concerns

None of the following are currently listed in the DSM-5. They are offered as observational concerns, not clinical diagnoses.

- **AI Psychosis** — distortion of what is real, what is original thinking, what the AI actually is
- **Tech-Induced Dissociation** — disconnection from thoughts, feelings, and body during or after AI use
- **De-realization** — the world feeling flat or unreal after sustained AI use
